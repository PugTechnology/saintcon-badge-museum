U22 CLUE NOTEPAD

A Clue-style notepad with 52 LEDs and no screen. A stand-in kiosk hands out the clues.

Play Original Firmware
Hardware and credits

RP2040 dual Cortex-M0+ (QSPI flash) • 52 charlieplexed RGB LEDs on GP0–GP14 (21 Suspects, 21 Attacks/Exploits, 10 Rooms) • UP / DOWN / SELECT tactile buttons (GP15–GP17) • Ambient light sensor (I2C0 @ 0x29) • Dual-wing minibadge connectors (8 total, 4 per wing) • Stand-in museum kiosk over firmware serial protocol.

Repo: compukidmike/saintcon2022 (383964e) • Licence not stated • Third-party licenses

About this museum

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SAINTCON or UtahSAINT.

SAINTCON is Utah's annual cybersecurity and hackers conference, organized by the UtahSAINT community and known for its custom electronic badges.

Browser-Only Storage: Each badge's simulated EEPROM/flash is stored locally in your browser's localStorage (isolated per year and mode). No external CDNs, no analytics, and no cookies are used.

Sources and licences

2017 (HITB Minibadge 2017), 2018, 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024, and 2025 are built from compukidmike's source (2017 third-party licenses, 2018 third-party licenses, 2019 third-party licenses, 2020 third-party licenses, 2023 third-party licenses, 2024 third-party licenses, 2025 third-party licenses); 2021 is built from the badge created by compukidmike, Professor_Plum, and Sodium_Hydrogen (2021 third-party licenses); 2026 is built from Professor-plum's MIT-licensed source (2026 third-party licenses).
