About

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SAINTCON or UtahSAINT.

SAINTCON is Utah's annual cybersecurity and hackers conference, celebrated for its custom hardware badges and conference-wide badge games.

This is a fan-made hardware emulator built with the badge author's (compukidmike) permission.

Privacy: All badge progress (simulated 512-byte EEPROM/NVS) is saved in your browser only (localStorage, isolated per year and mode) — no cookies, no analytics, and no external CDNs.
